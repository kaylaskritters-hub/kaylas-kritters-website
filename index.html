Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve?

We serve Richmond, Hope Valley, Wakefield, Kingston, Charlestown and West Kingston, Rhode Island.

What kinds of pets do you care for?

We care for all kinds of kritters! Our experience includes dogs, cats, fish and small animals. Have something a little different? Reach out and tell us about your pet and their care needs.

Do you provide overnight care?

Yes. In-home overnight care is available in 10-hour and 12-hour options. View rates.

Can you help with medications?

Yes. We can provide medication assistance when needed as part of our pet care services.

Are you insured?

Yes. Kayla's Kritters is insured and pet CPR certified.

Do you offer gift certificates?

Yes. Gift certificates are available. For inquiries, please contact us.
